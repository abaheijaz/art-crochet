Introduction

Welcome to my crochet space.

Assalamualaikum, Hi!!

I’m Hikmah (希玛 · xīmǎ), and I’ve been falling deeper into the world of crochet since 2022. I created this space to share my handmade pieces, along with the inspiration, tutorials, and patterns that have shaped my crochet journey—a small way of giving back to the community that taught me so much.

Inspired by a crochet blanket for my son, my love for crochet began with the beauty of its stitches. I now create handmade crochet pieces, each crafted with care and love.

Enjoy browsing, and don’t hesitate to reach out on WhatsApp , Instagram, and TikTok if you'd like to know more.
