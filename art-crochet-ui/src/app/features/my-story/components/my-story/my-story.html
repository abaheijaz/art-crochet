My Story

In the stillness of making, something quietly found me.

I still remember the first time I came across a colorful crochet baby blanket. I fell in love with it instantly.

At that time, I didn't even know what crochet was, but something about it made me curious. I started exploring, learning about stitches, and decided to give it a try.

What began as "this looks interesting" slowly grew into a passion.

When I crochet, I feel like I'm in my own little world. The noise fades, and everything feels calmer.

After losing my son in 2025, I found myself holding onto crochet and creative arts even more closely. It became a gentle space for healing and comfort, helping me through my grieving process one stitch at a time.

Today, I take limited orders, allowing each piece to be made slowly and with care. If you'd like to know more, feel free to reach out to me.

I'm sharing this story in the hope that it might encourage you to explore something you're curious about too. I started at 32, and I truly believe it's never too late to begin.
