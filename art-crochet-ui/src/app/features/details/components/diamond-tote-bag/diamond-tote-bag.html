auto_stories Backstory

I love patchwork designs, and at one point I was exploring granny squares with the idea of making a cardigan.

As I was still at the beginner stage of learning crochet, I found that joining the squares was a bit tedious and required more effort than I expected 🙂

Then I came across this stitch and instantly loved it. It let me play around with colors while still achieving the geometric, patchwork effect I was looking for, without needing to join multiple pieces 😉. Eventually, I found a tote bag tutorial and started practicing the technique. From there, I adapted and adjusted the design to suit my own style and preferences.
