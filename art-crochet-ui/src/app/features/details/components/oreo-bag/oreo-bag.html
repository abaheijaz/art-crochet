auto_stories Backstory

This cute bag was inspired by a reference photo shared by a friend. I later found a simple tutorial on YouTube and decided to give it a try.

My first version was made using milk cotton yarn in a smaller, child-sized design, paired with a longer pearl chain strap.

Over time, I refined and improvised the design by creating a larger version using sturdier yarn, making it more durable and practical for everyday use. I also introduced two strap options for better versatility and comfort. To achieve its signature look, I used two contrasting yarn colors to create a more Oreo-like appearance.
