auto_stories Backstory

The 3 Layer Tote Bag started as a custom request after a friend shared a reference photo of a crochet tote bag. With no pattern or tutorial available, the design was developed entirely through hands-on exploration, careful adjustments, and repeated trials to achieve the right balance of form and proportion.

The three layers refer to three stacked colors, creating a simple yet eye-catching look. Made using basic stitches, the tote was designed to stay minimal, practical, and easy to carry, letting the colors speak for themselves.

At the same time, some prefer a more understated aesthetic. For those drawn to simplicity, the tote is also made in a single color, with beige being a favorite for its clean, calm, and timeless feel. This design eventually became the base idea for the Mini Tote Bag, a smaller and more accessible version for everyday use.
