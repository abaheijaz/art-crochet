auto_stories Backstory

While browsing the internet for a new pattern to work on, I stumbled upon this crochet bucket hat pattern by Sarah Maker. I tried it using yarn I already had at home and instantly fell in love with the result.

The pattern is available in sizes ranging from baby to adult XL. Personally, I prefer the size L, as it is the most comfortable to wear over a hijab and has a slightly loose, relaxed fit.

I have not tried the baby sizes yet, as I am still searching for a suitable yarn that works well with Malaysia's warm weather.
